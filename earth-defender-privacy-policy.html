Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how JEB Studios (“we”, “us”), operated by Jairo Emilio Bonilla Cálix, handles information in the mobile game Earth Defender (the “App”), available on Google Play.

Information we store on your device

Earth Defender saves game progress locally on your device using platform storage (SharedPreferences). This may include:

  • Coins and upgrade levels
  • Highest level cleared and collected power-ups
  • Audio and vibration settings
  • Ad grace-period flag (whether tutorial ads have been enabled)

We do not require an account, and we do not operate our own servers to collect or store your gameplay data in the current version of the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements:

  • Banner ads appear on menu screens (main menu, level select, workshop, settings) after your first completed run. They do not appear during active gameplay.
  • Rewarded video ads are optional. You may choose to watch an ad after a run to earn bonus coins.

AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) to deliver, measure, and personalize ads. Google’s use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device settings (Google Settings → Ads on Android).

Third-party services

Service Purpose
Google AdMob Display and measure advertisements

Children’s privacy

Earth Defender is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App contains advertising and is intended for a general audience.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
