Privacy Policy for Earth Defender

Effective date: 2026-05-27

This Privacy Policy describes how Earth Defender ("the App") handles information. If you install or use the App, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1) Information we collect

The App is designed to work without requiring you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information.

Depending on how the App is built and distributed, the App and/or third‑party services may collect limited information automatically, such as:

  • Device information (model, OS version, language, identifiers).
  • App activity (screens viewed, basic interactions).
  • Diagnostics (crash logs, performance data).
  • Approximate location inferred from IP (if collected by third‑parties).

2) How we use information

  • To operate and improve the App.
  • To understand usage and fix bugs.
  • To provide optional features such as analytics, crash reporting, or advertising (when enabled).

3) Third‑party services

The App may use third‑party services (for example, platform services like Google Play Services and, if enabled, analytics/crash/ads providers). These services may collect information as described in their own privacy policies.

Note: The exact third‑party services used depend on the specific build you install.

4) Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only as necessary to operate the App (for example, through third‑party services described above) or when required by law.

5) Data retention

If any analytics or crash logs are collected, they are retained only as long as needed for debugging and improving the App, and according to the retention rules of the providers used.

6) Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7) Children’s privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

8) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
