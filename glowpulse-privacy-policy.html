Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how JEB Studios (“we”, “us”) handles information in the mobile app GlowPulse (the “App”), a classic Tetris-style puzzle game with a synthwave neon aesthetic, available on Google Play under the package name com.jebstudios.glowpulse.

Summary

GlowPulse is a fully offline game. We do not require an account, we do not display advertisements, we do not use analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not operate servers to collect your data. All gameplay runs on your device.

Information we store on your device

The App saves a small amount of data locally using Android SharedPreferences. This includes:

  • High score — your best game score, stored as an integer
  • Audio preference — whether sound is muted, stored as a boolean

This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us or any third party. Uninstalling the App removes this data from your device.

Network use

GlowPulse does not make network requests during normal use. The App works entirely offline. Fonts and audio assets are bundled with the App and are not fetched from external servers at runtime.

Device permissions (Android)

The release version of the App does not request sensitive permissions such as Internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage. The App uses standard Android haptic feedback for gameplay interactions; no additional vibration permission is required on modern Android versions.

Third-party libraries

The App uses open-source libraries for game rendering, audio playback, and local storage. These libraries process data only on your device and do not send information to external services:

Library Purpose
Flame Game engine for rendering and gameplay
Flame Audio / Audioplayers Playback of bundled music and sound effects
Shared Preferences Local storage of high score and mute setting
Google Fonts (Orbitron) Typography — fonts are bundled locally; no runtime font downloads

Backups and data security

Android backup is enabled for the App. If you use Google or device backup on your phone, your high score and audio preference may be included in those backups. Backups are managed by Google or your device manufacturer, not by JEB Studios. We do not have access to backup data.

Children’s privacy

GlowPulse is suitable for all ages and is not directed at collecting personal information from anyone, including children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if we add online features, advertisements, or analytics in a future version. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
